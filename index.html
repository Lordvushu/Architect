Materiality

Material study.

We select timeless, natural materials that grow more attractive as they weather in our high-altitude sun and rain.

Dark Mountain Basalt

Permanent, grounded, and naturally insulating.

Sourced directly across Lesotho, rough-hewn basalt stone walls provide massive thermal mass—absorbing solar heat during winter afternoons and releasing it gently overnight.

Surface Character: Textured stone relief with mineral seams
Best Applied: Heavy thermal spine walls and external plinths
